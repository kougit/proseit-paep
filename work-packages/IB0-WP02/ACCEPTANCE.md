## Technical result rule

(i) **PASS** requires AC01–AC24 to pass.

(ii) Any failed mandatory criterion means the status remains IN PROGRESS or FAILED.

(iii) BLOCKED is permitted only when an external decision, an environment limitation or an architecture conflict prevents a legitimate fix. Examples: R1–R3 are not met, or no pinned Node is available.

(iv) Human **ACCEPTED** status may be recorded only after technical PASS. A licence disposition marked "Pending" does not block technical PASS if AC15 records it correctly. The accepting reviewer must decide it, or record it as a condition, before or at acceptance.
