# IB0-WP03 Acceptance Criteria

Every criterion is mandatory unless an authorised reviewer explicitly marks it N/A. Claude may not convert a mandatory criterion to N/A, weaken its wording, or replace its verification with a weaker one (PAEP-EQP-01 §23, "No test laundering").

"Baseline" means the IB0-WP02 merge commit `de13629` on `main`. "Pinned toolchain" means Node 24.21.0 and npm 11.19.0 as recorded in `.nvmrc` and `package.json`. "Strict-family options" means `strict`, `noImplicitAny`, `strictNullChecks`, `strictFunctionTypes`, `strictBindCallApply`, `strictPropertyInitialization`, `strictBuiltinIteratorReturn`, `noImplicitThis`, `useUnknownInCatchVariables` and `alwaysStrict`. "Template strict options" means `strictTemplates`, `fullTemplateTypeCheck` and the `strictInputTypes`, `strictInputAccessModifiers`, `strictNullInputTypes`, `strictAttributeTypes`, `strictSafeNavigationTypes`, `strictDomLocalRefTypes`, `strictOutputEventTypes`, `strictDomEventTypes`, `strictContextGenerics` and `strictLiteralTypes` flags.

## Technical result rule

(i) **TECHNICAL PASS** requires AC01–AC24 to pass.

(ii) Any failed mandatory criterion leaves the status IN PROGRESS or FAILED.

(iii) BLOCKED is permitted only when an external decision, an environment limitation or an architecture conflict prevents a legitimate fix.

(iv) ACCEPTED may be recorded only by the Product Owner / Solutions Architect after technical PASS.
